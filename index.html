03 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PIXELS
A curious mind. A considered eye. A little instinct.
I’m Mara, an independent brand designer for people doing things a little differently. After eight years in London studios, I built a practice around the part I love most: finding the thing only you can be.
I believe the best work feels inevitable, not fashionable. It comes from paying attention, asking better questions and caring slightly too much about the details. Away from the desk: second-hand bookshops, long lunches and a camera I take everywhere.
